Size and adapter interpretation

The 120–170 bp raw-size window is supportive only. Dimer-like units have a ≤10-bp central gap. Unreconstructed template matches remain candidates; resolved 11–50 bp inserts are retained. Full-library sizes include expected outer adapters and inferred physical indexes, including units with undetected outer handles. The main profile weights each recovered unit by its modeled full-library length; count-based profiles are expandable. Curves are conditional on sequencing and adapter recovery and do not measure TapeStation fluorescence or NGS molarity.

Pairtools pair types · counts and methods

Reconstructed ONT inserts are viewed as overlapping terminal reads of at most 150 bp, filtered with fastp, aligned with BWA-MEM2 and classified by Pairtools. Results are shown after duplicate marking. Percentages use every parsed pair, including unmapped pairs and masked walks.

Pair types and analysis settings

UU: unique–unique; UR/RU: rescued single-ligation pairs; DD: coordinate duplicates marked within the sample; WW: masked complex walks; Other: remaining types, including unmapped or non-unique ends.

fastp paired-end adapter detection; bwa-mem2 mem -SP; Pairtools parse with MAPQ ≥1, walks-policy mask, maximum inter-alignment gap 20 bp and maximum molecule size 2,000 bp; duplicate marking with a 1-bp tolerance, max method and scipy backend. Duplicates stay in the denominator. Sublibraries inherit marks from the full sample.

These are parsing outcomes, not direct ligation counts or a calibrated library-quality score. DD depends on sampling depth and does not estimate PCR duplication or library complexity. The native valid-contact metrics use their own end geometry and filters. Pairtools definitions.

Assay interpretation

Micro-C endpoint composition is not assigned a biological success/failure label. The 200-contact minimum describes evidence count only. Cis and long-range cis use all unique valid contacts; Wilson 95% intervals assume independent contacts and do not include molecule dependence or recovery bias. Short inward pairs below 200 bp are excluded heuristically and may include real local contacts. Full-insert ligation structure is reported separately.

Methods & interpretation

Reference values distinguish local screening settings, biological context and statistical expectations. Gene profiles show mean coverage and SEM across annotated genes; SEM is not biological replicate uncertainty. CPM uses MAPQ-pass configured primary nuclear alignments in each scope. Micro-C chromosome density uses endpoint legs. Pairtools reports sample-level coordinate duplicate marks as DD; shallow sequencing does not estimate PCR duplication or library complexity. Native contact deduplication remains an internal validity filter.
